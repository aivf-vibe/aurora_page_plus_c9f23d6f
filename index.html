Frequently Asked Questions

What is Skynet?

Skynet is a comprehensive DeFi platform built on Web3 technology, offering services like yield farming, staking, DEX trading, and more.

How do I get started?

Simply connect your Web3 wallet (MetaMask, WalletConnect) to our platform and start exploring our DeFi services immediately.

Is Skynet secure?

Yes, all our smart contracts are audited by top security firms and we implement multiple security layers to protect user funds.

What are the fees?

We offer competitive fees starting at 0.1% for trading, with additional benefits for SKY token holders.

Do you have a token?

Yes, the SKY token is our native utility token used for governance, fee discounts, and rewards in our ecosystem.

How do I earn rewards?

You can earn rewards through yield farming, staking, providing liquidity, and participating in our governance system.
